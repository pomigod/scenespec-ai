QA 01PTS_TIME

            
VFR Passthrough Cadence Audit

            
Inspects showinfo pts_time frame deltas with -vsync passthrough to expose hidden variable-frame-rate stutter and duplicate hold frames.

          

          

            
QA 02MOTION BINS

            
0.5s Motion Bins & Spike Detection

            
Quantifies frame-to-frame pixel deltas across 0.5-second bins to catch frozen stretches (<0.8 diff) and sudden morphing/jump-cut spikes.

          

          

            
QA 03GEMINI 4

            
4x4 Micro-Grid Physics Gate

            
Extracts 16 sequential high-res frames around peak action cuts so Gemini 4 verifies object permanence, gravity, and zero prop teleportation.

          

          

            
QA 04TENENGRAD

            
Adaptive AMD CAS A/B Benchmark

            
Sweeps CAS strengths (0.14–0.24) per clip, selecting the exact point that maximizes Tenengrad edge clarity while bounding flat-surface noise.

          

          

            
QA 05EBU R128

            
Audio F0, -15.5 LUFS & <50ms Sync

            
Audits EBU R128 loudness (-15.5 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP), voice pitch bleed, 0.08s crossfade smoothing, blackdetect, and <50ms A/V sync drift.

Generates a 3fps full-clip contact sheet and 1-page X-Ray review board backed by SHA-256 segment caching for instant client approval.
