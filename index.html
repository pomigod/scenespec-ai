3-Stage Deterministic Pipeline

Eliminate prompt gacha before rendering and catch invisible audio-visual failures before publishing.

01

Gemini 4 Spec & 3s Motion Gate

Gemini 4 compiles scripts into 3-shot storyboards capped at 4 syllables/sec and flags static-lock hazards. OpenCV scores 360p drafts at 0.4–4.8s via outer-rim MAE, 1-SSIM, and Farneback optical flow before promoting to 720p or 4K.

02

Deterministic FFmpeg Director

Generative models ignore camera-move prompts 50–70% of the time. CutGate shifts sub-pixel push-ins, commercial S-curve LUTs, and audio-tail fades into exact FFmpeg 7.1 filtergraphs for 0% drift across renders.

03

6-Defect Forensics & X-Ray Board

Every stitched cut is audited for F0 voice bleed, late reverse-zoom morphing, cut flashes, and AI-disclosure compliance, then summarized into a 1-page color pop-out X-Ray proof board for 3-second human sign-off.

6-Point Forensic Audio-Visual QA Module

          
Zero-trust verification built from real post-production failures: never ship unverified AI video again.

        

        

          

            
QA 1

            
Voice F0 Gender & Pitch Bleed

            
Bandpass + autocorrelation F0 tracking verifies narrator pitch consistency (165–350 Hz) and catches sudden male/off-tone voice bleed (<150 Hz).

          

          

            
QA 2

            
Audio Tail Margin Guard

            
Scans the final 6.5–10.0s buffer window of every clip for RMS spikes, syllable overflow, and boundary clipping pops before stitching.

          

          

            
QA 3

            
Late Reverse-Zoom & Freeze

            
Radial optical flow over 07.0–10.0s flags generative bounce-back artifacts, sudden frame freezes, and morphing spikes.

          

          

            
QA 4

            
Cut-Boundary Flash & Drift

            
Measures luma (YAVG) dips and spikes across every 10s clip boundary to catch black frames, white flashes, and crossfade timing drift.

          

          

            
QA 5

            
AI Disclosure & Endcard Audit

            
Verifies 0–3s AI-generated disclosure placement and final 55–63s brand endcard black purity, contrast, and exact resolution via FFprobe.

          

          

            
QA 6

            
3x Loupe & 1-Page X-Ray Board

            
Extracts 1.5s, 4.5s, and 8.0s 3x-zoomed detail crops into a single color-coded X-Ray approval sheet with SHA-256 integrity hashes.
